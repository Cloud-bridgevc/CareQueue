Questions

Is there an onboarding fee?

Essentials includes self-serve onboarding. Professional and Enterprise include a guided onboarding: kickoff, SSO setup, data import, staff training and a 30-day adoption review.

Do AI summaries cost extra?

No. Summary drafting is included on Professional and Enterprise. Drafts are always labelled and reviewed by your staff before they are shared.

Can we import our existing schedules?

Yes — CSV import for caregivers, clients and recurring visits, plus API access on Professional and above.

Does CareQueue replace our EVV system?

No. CareQueue reads confirmations from your EVV or telephony provider and monitors for gaps. It can also record manual office confirmations with a required reason.
