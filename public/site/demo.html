What the rules check

  • Overlaps — the same caregiver on two visits at once.
  • Availability and time off — compared to recorded weekly windows and leave.
  • Credentials — service requirements vs. the caregiver record.
  • Coverage — unassigned visits inside your coverage horizon.
  • Travel — gap between consecutive visits vs. zone estimate plus buffer.
  • Weekly hours — scheduled hours vs. each caregiver's limit.
Explainable by design.

Detection is deterministic: the same schedule always produces the same findings, each traceable to a configurable rule. That makes it auditable — and keeps judgment calls with your coordinators.
