The problem

Every missed confirmation starts as a gap nobody saw in time.

Home-care agencies run on thin margins, high turnover and long lists of people waiting for care. Schedules change hourly — and the records that prove a visit happened arrive late, or not at all.

Published figures from third-party sources, linked above. They describe the pressure on home care, not CareQueue results. CareQueue is administrative software and gives no clinical advice.

How a missed confirmation reaches a supervisor

A worked example from the demonstration agency.

Visit ends

A 2-hour companion visit is scheduled to end at 2:00 PM. No check-in or check-out arrives from the caregiver's app.

Grace period passes

At 2:30 PM the confirmation monitor sees the visit is still unconfirmed past the agency's 30-minute grace period.

Queue item created

A critical item enters the supervisor queue with the rule, the record it relied on and a 2-hour follow-up target.

Supervisor resolves

The supervisor calls the caregiver, records a manual confirmation with a reason, and closes the item with a note. Every step is audit-logged.

Designed for privacy from the start

Coordination tools touch sensitive information. CareQueue keeps it minimal and controlled.

  • Clients are shown by ID and initials; addresses stay in your secure access record.
  • Hash-chained audit log of every change and every sensitive view.
  • Configurable retention per record type.
  • Sales tools never receive care information.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask

✉ Email the founders
Does CareQueue make clinical decisions?

No. CareQueue flags administrative and scheduling gaps for your staff to act on. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment or assess patient safety, and care observations are routed to your clinical lead.

What problem does it actually solve?

Schedules change constantly and the records proving a visit happened arrive late. CareQueue watches both, raises conflicts and missing confirmations early, and gives supervisors one prioritised queue with owners and due times.

Does it replace our EVV or payroll system?

No. It works alongside them. The pilot covers importing schedules and records; integrations are scoped per agency.

How are AI summaries controlled?

They are drafts only, labelled as such and hidden until an authorised supervisor approves them. The records used are listed with every draft.

Who can see what?

Role-based access: caregivers see only their own visits, restricted notes need an extra grant, and every change and sensitive view is audit-logged.

How is client information protected?

Clients are shown by ID and initials, addresses stay in your secure access record, and sales tools never receive care information.

How much does it cost?

See the pricing page. Prices are illustrative and exclude taxes; early-access agencies are onboarded by the founders.

Is it available today?

CareQueue is in early access. The demo runs on a synthetic agency; a pilot runs on yours. Request a demo and a founder will reply personally.
